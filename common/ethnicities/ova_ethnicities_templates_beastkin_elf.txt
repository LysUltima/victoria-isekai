# Elven-faced variants of the human ethnicity templates used by the beastkin.
# Each inherits its human base (skin tone, eye/hair colour, hairstyles, beards,
# human ears) unchanged and overrides ONLY the signature elven bone-structure
# genes: narrow, sharply defined jaw; high, forward cheekbones with little cheek
# fat; a small, slim, straight nose; upward-slanted eyes; a narrow chin and head;
# and high, arched brows. Values are taken from the moon_elf template. These are
# universal human face-morph genes, so they apply cleanly on the human head mesh.
# Elf-specific traits (pointed mer-ears, elven hairstyles/eyebrows, elf ageing)
# are deliberately NOT copied: the beastkin keep their own animal ears and fur-
# matched hair.
