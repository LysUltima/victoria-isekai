### Isekai regional home-front flavor events (Agent C, sole owner of this file)
# Namespace ova_isekai_flavor. Not storyline-specific, so it no longer depends on a journal
# entry: fired directly from the regular on_action isekai_flavor_monthly_events
# (common/on_actions/ova_flavor_on_actions.txt), hooked into the engine's on_monthly_pulse_country
# (common/on_actions/00_code_on_actions.txt) - do not renumber these fixed ids, the on_action's
# weights are wired against them. Each event's own trigger now carries the eligibility that used
# to live on je_ova_isekai_home_front's is_shown_when_inactive (human nation, capital in the
# isekai region, while the Demonic Empire still exists and the war still burns) - not champion
# events (champion XP/renown is Agent A/B's ova_hero namespace). Uses only verified
# vanilla effects/triggers plus the ova_isekai_flavor_* modifiers defined in
# common/static_modifiers/ova_isekai_flavor_modifiers.txt.
#
#  .1  refugee column from the marches         (war economy: who pays)
#  .2  hero-mania among the youth
#  .3  war-orphan charity drive
#  .4  the Church's tithe collectors            (war economy: who profits)
#  .5  mercenary boom                           (war economy: who profits)
#  .6  border panic (false alarm)
#  .7  the profiteers' banquet                  (war economy: who profits)
#  .8  pilgrimage to a summoning site
#  .9  a demon peddler in town
#  .10 veterans come home changed               (war economy: who pays)
#  .11 the relic auction                        (war economy: who profits)
#  .12 "not our war" pamphlets
#  .13 rumor of a miracle at the front
#  .14 letters from the front, read aloud in the square
