# ova_xianxia.10-.14   archetype intros (check-in / cauldron / grandpa / returner / reader)
# ova_xianxia.15-.19   chapter beats shared with the old rise set (.16/.17/.18 ch1, .19/.15 ch2)
# ova_xianxia.20-.24   chapter 3 archetype rung - the gift matures (self-gated on mc_type)
# ova_xianxia.25-.29   chapter 1 archetype rung - the gift stirs (self-gated on mc_type)
# ova_xianxia.30       the yearly check-in (type 1 only, self-rescheduling)

# ova_xianxia.50-.52   rival Destined Ones
# ova_xianxia.55-.58   the war layer (banner JE pulses)
# ova_xianxia.60-.62   outing outcomes (secret realm, tribulation)
# ova_xianxia.63       the realm breaks open (chapter completion celebration)
# ova_xianxia.64-.65   the grand competition, the ascension epilogue
# ova_xianxia.66-.68   goal decisions: secret manual / natal weapon / tribulation treasures
# ova_xianxia.70-.72   endings
# ova_xianxia.80-.84   cheat consults (type-branched)
# ova_xianxia.85-.89   chapter 5 archetype rung - the gift bares its depth
# ova_xianxia.100-.109 realm chapter beats (intros and finales)
# ova_xianxia.130-.135 hidden chapter relay events (call the tick ladders off-pulse)
# All champion XP/renown/effects are called from the foundation (ova_champion_*) or defined in
# the xianxia support files - never redefined here. Beats set ova_xianxia_seen_* flags in their
# immediate and refuse to fire twice; the tick ladders in
# common/scripted_effects/ova_xianxia_effects.txt walk them in order.
