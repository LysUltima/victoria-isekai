### Reyorn harem storyline - narrative events (John Paradox)
# A summoned otherworlder Hero courts, one state at a time, the beastkin woman
# of each of Reyorn's five provinces. Every courtship is EIGHT date beats: an
# intro that creates the heroine and seven more dates, each offering three
# choices that raise Affection by different amounts (best +5 / good +3 / poor
# +1). Once the Affection bar is full (50: eight best dates give 40, so at least
# one objective is always needed) the confession BUTTON on the journal lights up
# (ova_rey_buttons.txt); clicking it fires her confession event, and accepting
# any option there is the sole thing that wins her. Fall short and John moves on:
# if after all eight dates affection is still under 20 the courtship fails at
# once, and either way a spent five-year clock simply sends him to the next state.
#
# EVERY option carries exactly one gameplay effect on top of its affection, and
# that effect is THE LITERAL CONSEQUENCE OF WHAT THE OPTION TEXT SAYS HE DID. It
# is never a generic payout: help Nasira break the cartel and her spice routes
# reopen (a Katzenburg trade modifier); pay the deserter's fine and the treasury
# pays it; burn Fenna's manuscript and the republic loses the knowledge in it;
# gawk at Ursla in her own forge and the forge hands turn radical. The effect
# should always be guessable from the option line alone. Where the beat is
# purely private, the consequence is her own people's reaction in her own state
# (effect_ova_rey_state_loyalists / _radicals, keyed to her culture).
#
# There is no fixed reward curve any more, so there is nothing to pattern-match,
# but three balance rules hold across the whole chain:
#
#  1. THE BEST (+5) OPTION USUALLY COSTS HIM SOMETHING REAL - coin (outbidding an
#     auction for her, back-paying a season of accounts he was never charged for),
#     credit (feeding Fenna the fact that wins her debate), knowledge (trading away
#     a secret of his own world) or blood. EIGHT beats wound him via
#     ova_champion_injure, which locks the champion out of adventures for months
#     and is tooltipped at the option: .13.a stepping in front of bandit blades (3),
#     .53.a putting himself between Fenna and the thing in the archive (3), .23.b
#     walking into a grain riot alone (2), .33.a taking a mine beam on his shoulders
#     (2), .22.a letting Bruna put him in the dirt at full strength (1), .31.a going
#     all out against a bearkin arm (1), .35.a steadying the tongs through a molten
#     pour (1), .43.b a night chest-deep in snowmelt damming the weir (1).
#  2. THE POOR (+1) OPTION MUST BE TEMPTING, NOT A TRAP. It is the pragmatic,
#     professional or frankly mercenary choice, and it PAYS: coin (skim her ledger,
#     sell the map, sell her cart out in an hour, take the arm-wrestling stakes), a
#     state gain (review the ranks like a general and every fault does get fixed),
#     bureaucracy (ask about the promotion paperwork and the paperwork gets filed),
#     innovation (explain the lantern physics), or the practical outcome anyway in a
#     lesser form (tell her the ledgers can wait and it still gets untangled, slower).
#     Only four beats are pure sting with no upside, and all four are genuine rudeness
#     (.22.c, .27.c, .41.c, .44.c) at very_small magnitude.
#  3. XP AND RENOWN ARE EARNED BY THE OPTION'S OWN CONTENT, never handed out for
#     standing there. XP only where he trains, fights, climbs, forges, labours or
#     studies for real; renown only where a crowd actually witnesses something
#     impressive (out-haggling Nasira in the open bazaar, holding Ursla to a draw in
#     a packed hall, facing a riot alone, winning a public debate). A tone-deaf
#     option never earns renown FOR being tone-deaf.
#
# The best option is still shuffled between the a/b/c slots beat to beat. The
# state-scope boilerplate lives in effect_ova_rey_state_* (ova_rey_effects.txt) and
# the modifiers in common/static_modifiers/ova_rey_modifiers.txt.
#
# ova_rey.1          John Paradox is summoned (prologue, creates the champion)
# ova_rey.10-.17     Katzenburg  - Nasira, the desert-cat merchant heiress (8 dates)
# ova_rey.18         ... her confession (fired by the journal button)
# ova_rey.19         ... she is won (from je on_complete)
# ova_rey.20-.27     Welpenstadt - Bruna, the dogkin guard-captain (8 dates)
# ova_rey.28 / .29   ... confession / won
# ova_rey.30-.37     Barenberg   - Ursla, the bearkin mountain smith (8 dates)
# ova_rey.38 / .39   ... confession / won
# ova_rey.40-.47     Hasenhafen  - Lieselotte, the rabbitkin harvest maiden (8 dates)
# ova_rey.48 / .49   ... confession / won
# ova_rey.50-.57     Fuschtaal    - Fenna, the foxkin scholar (8 dates)
# ova_rey.58 / .59   ... confession / won
# ova_rey.78         John has died (fail ending, extremely rare - he is plot-armored)
# ova_rey.80         a courtship ended cold; she moves on, and so does he
# ova_rey.90         the capstone: standing for the presidency
# ova_rey.91         the capstone epilogue
# ova_rey.94         hidden beat relay (fires the next unseen date mid-year)
#
# Each date sets an ova_rey_seen_NN flag in its immediate and refuses to fire
# twice; the eighth date of each girl also sets ova_rey_all_dates_<x> (the
# under-20 fail gate). All champion XP / renown flows through the foundation
# (ova_champion_*); all affection flows through effect_ova_rey_add_affection.
